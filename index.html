Privacy

Your location is worked out on your own device and is never sent anywhere or stored: GPS, compass and step counting are processed inside the app only, and nothing is kept when you turn live location off.

The app has no analytics or trackers. If you connect Outlook, it reads only today's meetings (subject, time, room) directly from Microsoft to suggest a room; no calendar data leaves your device. Your sign-in stays on your device and you can revoke it at any time in your Microsoft account.

The static website host (for example Netlify or GitHub Pages) sees normal web traffic such as your IP address, like any website. It never receives your position.
