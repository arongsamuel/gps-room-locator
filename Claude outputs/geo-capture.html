Tips for accurate points
  • Use the outline of the building. Outer corners and the ends of long outer walls give the best rotation and scale. Points near the middle add little.
  • Spread them out. Two points on opposite corners are the minimum. 3–4 points let the fit check itself, using the residuals shown above.
  • Stand near windows on the floor you're calibrating, and keep the phone still for the full 30 s. Your screen stays on during the lock.
  • Turn on precise location. iPhone: Settings → Privacy → Location → your browser → Precise. Android: Google Location Accuracy on, and keep Wi-Fi on.
  • Expect ±3–10 m indoors. The spread figure shows how stable the lock was. Redo a point if the spread is above ~8 m.
